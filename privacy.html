Privacy policy

What we keep

Your account ID, display name and picture; your balances, listings, deals and chat messages; the pictures you send; your boss calls, vouches and reports; which devices are logged in (a token, stored as a hash); and what the AI was asked and answered.

Signing in with Google

If you choose Google sign-in, we receive your Google account ID, name, email address and profile picture. We use them only to create your account, log you in and show your name and picture in the app. We do not read your Gmail, contacts, files or anything else in your Google account. We do not sell this information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Signing in with Discord

If you use your Discord account, we keep your Discord ID, display name and avatar link. We do not receive your Discord password.

Using the app as a guest

A guest has no account. We do not ask a guest for a name or an email address.

Languages and AI

The app is translated by an AI service (Groq). Only the texts of the app itself are sent to it, never your name, your messages or anything you wrote. Questions you ask the app's AI are sent to the AI provider so they can be answered.

Where it lives

On the bot's own server, not in a third-party analytics tool. The app on your device keeps your login token, your settings and a saved copy of the pages you opened so they can be read offline. Logging out removes that saved copy.

Who sees it

Other members see your name, picture, trust profile, listings and chat messages. Your email address is never shown to other members. Staff can see deal rooms, reports, pictures waiting for approval and AI questions. Owners can see which accounts exist, including the email address of Google accounts, to run the service and handle abuse.

Public game data

Online counts and public character and guild pages come from the game's public website. Descriptions of monsters and items also use text from the Rucoy fan wiki (Fandom), shared under CC BY-SA, and drop and item numbers from rucoystats.com (a fan site). We do not read or change anything inside the game, and we never ask for your game password.

Your choices

You can log out at any time and log out your other devices. To delete your account, your data or your messages, ask staff in the Discord server. You can also remove the app's access from your Google account settings at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page.
